Kubernetes and Azure Red Hat OpenShift, hands-on

From zero containers to an Azure Red Hat OpenShift (ARO) cluster. Every chapter ends with a lab that you run on your own Azure subscription, and a script that checks your work, stage by stage, and tells you what to fix.

The course is written for people who know basic Azure (the portal, subscriptions, resource groups, az login, virtual networks at a high level) and have never used containers or Kubernetes. Start with the Introduction: it explains how the course works and what it costs, before the setup.

Cost: about 1.50 EUR for the setup and chapters 1 and 2, and 8 to 9 EUR per three-hour ARO session in chapters 8 and 9 (chapters 3 to 7 are not priced yet). What the course costs and how to keep it low.
